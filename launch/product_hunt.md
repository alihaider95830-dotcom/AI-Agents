Hey PH! 👋

I built Studio because I kept spending 3-4 hours manually doing research that should take minutes.

The core idea: instead of one AI doing everything poorly, use a team of specialised agents - each with a specific job:

🔍 Researcher - searches 8-12 live sources, stores findings in a vector database
📋 Planner - reads the research, builds an outline with arguments per section
✍️ Writer - drafts the full report with inline citations
✅ QA - cross-references every claim against the source list

The whole pipeline runs in under 90 seconds and produces a downloadable PDF.

Free plan gives you 2 reports/month forever - no card required.

Happy to answer anything about the tech stack or how the agents work. Would love your feedback! 🙏
